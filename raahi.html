For drivers

Why drivers switch to Raahi

An electric auto pays off when it is busy all day. Saathi keeps it busy with booked riders.

Spend less to run it

Electric autos cost 38-46% less to run than CNG autos in a CEEW study in Lucknow (CEEW).

Booked riders on every trip

On Saathi routes, riders book ahead, so you never wait at the stand for the auto to fill. A booked seat is a paid seat, even if a rider does not show.

Keep what you earn

No commission on Saathi rides, only a small daily fee. The 2025 rules ask ride apps to give drivers at least 80% of the fare (Aggregator Guidelines 2025).

Insured while you drive

Ride apps must give each driver ₹5 lakh health insurance and ₹10 lakh term insurance under the 2025 rules (Aggregator Guidelines 2025).

A safer cabin

Reports on Raahi show doors, a large windshield and mirrors on the front pillars (Cartoq; 91trucks).

Service near you

Ola is targeting more than 500 dealer stores, for sales, service and test rides (Inc42).

Already drive a CNG or petrol auto? Upgrade to Raahi and we value your old auto at your doorstep. Cities are moving this way: in Delhi, only electric autos can be newly registered from 1 January 2027, and any replacement must be electric (Business Today 2026).

  • Doors that close, live trip sharing and an SOS button
  • The option to share only with women, for verified women riders
  • Timed to your train, with a free ride if Saathi makes you miss it
